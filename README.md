# Cake — v1.50 (fillings flow out over naked sides)

The tag reads **v1.50**.

On a naked cake each filling layer now has a real EDGE — one flowing sheet, as in the mock-ups —
replacing the recessed groove:

- **Creamy**: a stiff bead, a little uneven, standing just proud of the sponge where more was squeezed
  out — the icing's satin.
- **Glossy** and **Rich**: a sheet tucked under the sponge above that sags onto the sponge below in soft
  waves and a few broad lobes, merged by a smooth union so the edge is one continuous curve; its lower
  edge thickened and rounded like poured paint; no drips. Glossy is the thinner, lighter sheet.

The sheets are built over the tier's arc (so a cut cake's too), with the filling's own material, and
merged with the tier's stack before the sponge's baked wobble, so they move with the sponge and hug it.
They're not built under a scraped (semi-naked) coat, or on iced tiers, where they'd be hidden.

Checked in the harness: a naked cake with each type (see the image); no errors; the six saved links
decode unchanged. Not yet checked: a cut slice's edges, and Shuffle's filling picks (both slow in the
test browser — worth a look on the phone).
