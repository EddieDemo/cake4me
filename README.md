# Cake — v1.37 (metal number toppers, in any colour)

The tag reads **v1.37**.

**A Finish for number toppers: Wax | Metal**, in the Toppers tray under Style (dimmed until there's
a number). Metal is a polished metallic material with the same studio reflections as the gold and
silver candles. Its tint is the topper colour, set any way the wax can be: the presets, Recent,
the picker, the dropper — so gold, silver, rose gold, or metallic teal. Choosing a Style brings its
natural finish (Classic → Metal, Fun → Wax); Finish changes it after. It travels in the link
(`tm`: 0 Wax, 1 Metal; a cake without it is wax).

**New topper colours**, metal-friendly and fine in wax too: Rich gold (the gold candles' gold),
Silver, Rose gold, Champagne, Copper — added after the existing ones.

**Shuffle:** Classic numbers come mostly in metal, usually a classic metal (gold, silver, rose
gold, champagne or copper), otherwise in the theme's tint.

Checked in the harness: tapping Classic switches the finish to Metal; Rich gold, Silver, Rose gold
and a custom teal render as metal, with wax gold beside them for comparison, and Fun numbers in
metal work too; the dropper on a metal topper reads its exact tint; no errors; the six saved links
decode unchanged.
