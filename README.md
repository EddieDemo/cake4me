# Cake — v1.47 (bigger cakes: 10″ and 12″ tins, tiers up to 6″ deep)

The tag reads **v1.47**.

**Wider and deeper.** Width now goes to **10″ and 12″** tins (3″–12″; 12″ is 30 cm) and height to
**4″, 5″ and 6″** deep tiers (1″–6″; a 6″ tier is a "double barrel"). Each still has nine sizes, so
the link keeps one digit per size. A three-tier 12″·9″·6″ cake with 6″ tiers stands 46 cm tall.

**Big cakes keep their shadows and their studio.** The key light's shadow box and Studio's lamp were
sized for the largest cake of the time (9″, three 3½″ tiers). A bigger cake now scales them with it,
so its top still casts shadows (even in Low sun's long shadows) and the lamp still stands above it;
anything up to the old largest keeps scale 1, exactly as before. The soft-shadow calibration stays as
compiled (no shader rebuild), so a very big cake's shadow edges are a touch softer. The camera, the
gift box and the floor's glow already sized themselves to the cake.

**Shuffle** picks realistic sizes: bottom tiers of 5–10″ tins, 2½–4″ deep; upper tiers 2–3″ deep,
one to three tin sizes narrower. The builder offers the full range.

Checked in the harness: the Classic against v1.46 (unchanged: an average pixel change of 0.13 in 255);
a 12″ cake; a 12″·9″·6″ cake with 6″ tiers in Low sun and Studio (see the sheet); the new labels (10″ · 25 cm,
12″ · 30 cm; 4″ · 10 cm, 5″ · 13 cm, 6″ · 15 cm); no errors; the six saved links decode unchanged.

Changed files: `palettes.js`, `look.js`, `app.js`, `index.html`, `README.md`.
