# Cake — v1.51 (fillings: uneven layers, a wandering edge, pressed flat under ribbons)

The tag reads **v1.51**.

**Pressed flat under a ribbon.** Since v1.50 a filling's flowing edge could stand out further than a
ribbon and poke through it. Now, wherever a tier's ribbon covers a filling, the filling is pressed flat
beneath the band, easing back out just above and below it — as a ribbon tied round a naked cake does.

**A wandering edge (reach).** Spread by hand, a filling's outline wanders both ways: a few millimetres
short of the edge in places (the sponges nearly meet there, the filling set back in the gap), flush in
others, over the edge elsewhere. It only bulges, sags and waves where it overhangs. Buttercream does
the same, stiffly.

**Uneven layers.** A spread filling is never level — spooned honey, Nutella spread thick. Each layer's
thickness wanders round the cake (Creamy ±40%, Glossy ±50%, Rich ±45%); the sponge above rests on it,
its underside following the filling and its top a little less (soft sponge squashes over a thick spot);
the top stays level. It's one height remap of the whole tier — sponges, fillings, their edges and cut
faces alike — so nothing can disagree. (Naked tiers only; not under a scraped coat.)
